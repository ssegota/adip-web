About the Observatory

The Observatory in Pula is located in Monte Zaro Park, on the site of the former Austro-Hungarian Hydrographic Institute. Since 1973, it has served as the center of astronomical activities in Istria.

Our members act in visual observations, astrophotography, meteor tracking, and popularization of astronomy among citizens.
