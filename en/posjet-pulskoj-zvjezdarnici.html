Visit Pula Observatory

In order for the Astronomical Society "Istra" to successfully conduct its core activity, the popularization of astronomy and related sciences, visits by interested individuals and groups are organized at Pula Observatory.

Call +385 91 214 1966 Send an e-mail

Important note: Visiting the Observatory is currently possible exclusively by prior appointment.

What to expect

Depending on the age of the visitors, a visit generally includes:

Multimedia lecture about the universe

A lecture about the universe, adapted to the age of the visitors.

Observatory history and our activities

Learn about the history of Pula Observatory and the current activities of the Astronomical Society "Istra".

Telescope observation

Depending on weather conditions and the objects visible at that moment.

How to arrange a visit

Visits are organized for interested individuals and groups, exclusively by prior appointment.

  1. Get in touchAnnounce your visit by phone or e-mail.
  2. Agree on a dateFor groups, let us know the number and age of visitors so the programme can be adapted.
  3. Come to the ObservatoryPark Monte Zaro 2, Pula. Telescope observation depends on the weather.
